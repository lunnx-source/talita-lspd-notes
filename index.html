How to Use 10-Codes / Response Codes on Radio 🚨

[span_66](end_span)

Patroli Reporting:
[span_67](start_span)"Dispatch, this is 72ADAM505 doing 10-08 at municipal area, ready for any situation."

[span_67](end_span)

Updating reporting dari 55 berubah menjadi pengejaran 57:
[span_68](start_span)"Dispatch, for the last 10-55 situation on [nama jalan], it's now changing into active 10-57 (pursuit). Requesting 10-78 (backup) on my 20 (location). Suspect is now heading (arah pelarian)."

[span_68](end_span) [span_69](start_span)

Detail: "Suspect is now heading West on Vespucci Blvd, taking left, heading South on San Andreas Ave."

Pursuit Behavior - Unit Roles ⭐

    [span_94](start_span)

    • [span_95](end_span) [span_96](start_span)

Incident Commander (IC) Protocols 📢
[span_99](end_span)

Menurut Talita, sebagai Incident Commander (IC) hal yang terpenting adalah tegas. [span_100](start_span)IC diberikan kewenangan tertinggi dalam suatu insiden, sehingga segala hal yang terjadi harus dalam sepersetujuan IC.

[span_100](end_span) [span_101](start_span)

Tanggung Jawab IC di Crime Scene

[span_101](end_span)
    [span_102](start_span)
  • Wajib double check apakah seluruh evidence telah terkumpul (crime scene evidence, run plate, dsb).
    • [span_102](end_span) [span_103](start_span)
  • Menentukan pursuit line up: siapa yang akan menjadi primary, secondary, alternate, dan juga securing hostage.
    • [span_103](end_span) [span_104](start_span)
  • Catat semua kejadian dan jangan lupa melengkapi mdt selengkap lengkapnya.
    • [span_104](end_span)
[span_105](start_span)

Selama Pengejaran

[span_105](end_span)
    [span_106](start_span)
  • Sigap atas segala perubahan dan tindakan suspect.
    • [span_106](end_span) [span_107](start_span)
  • Jika tindakan suspect membahayakan warga atau sudah demand rules, IC harus memperhitungkan penggunaan spike trap atau PIT maneuver.
    • [span_107](end_span) [span_108](start_span)
  • Take tire hanya dilakukan jika situasi sudah tidak kondusif.
    • [span_108](end_span)
[span_109](start_span)

Komunikasi & Ketegasan

[span_109](end_span)
    [span_110](start_span)
  • Jangan takut menegur unit yang melakukan kesalahan.
    • [span_110](end_span) [span_111](start_span)
  • Jika ada officer yang menginterupsi radio dengan informasi tidak bermanfaat, IC harus tegas mengatakan "10-3".
    • [span_111](end_span)
[span_112](start_span)

Setelah Situasi Terkendali

[span_112](end_span)
    [span_113](start_span)
  • Pastikan semua suspect diproses sesuai prosedur.
    • [span_113](end_span) [span_114](start_span)
  • Pastikan MDT (Mobile Data Terminal) diselesaikan dengan rapi dan lengkap.
    • [span_114](end_span)
